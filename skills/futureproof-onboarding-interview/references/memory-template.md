# FutureProof Memory — output skeleton

> The synthesized output of `/futureproof-onboarding-interview` (Stage 4). Fill it in the **client's own words**, not marketing-speak. This is what saves into FutureProof and becomes the connective tissue every FutureProof skill reads. Each section maps to one area of the Stage-3 coverage map. Leave a section's "still thin" flag on if the interview didn't clear its bar — honesty here makes every downstream skill better.

---

## 1. What the business does
_On a Tuesday, plain English, no category labels. A stranger should be able to picture the actual work._

## 2. Why it exists
_The problem it solves + why the existing options weren't good enough. A specific inadequacy, not "they were bad."_

## 3. Who it serves
_One real human, not a segment: their state on arrival, the words they use, their fears, their wins, what they tried before. You could spot them in a room._

## 4. What ships
_The real deliverable + the process behind it. What a client actually gets for their money — a concrete artifact + the steps._

## 5. Where it's going
_Success at 12 months and at 3 years. What would let the founder walk away happy. A number or a named outcome._

## 6. What it stands for
_The non-negotiables. What it won't do even for money — a real line they've actually held._

## 7. What makes it different
_The version they'd tell a competitor over a beer. Not repeatable by a rival._

## 8. How it sounds (voice)
_Tone, signature phrases, banned words. You could ghostwrite a sentence they'd approve._

## 9. How it operates (team & ops)
_Team, roles, the processes that work vs. the ones that live in someone's head. Named owners + where it breaks._

## 10. Where the gaps are
_What slows it down, what's undocumented, the founder-shaped ceiling. An honest weak point, not a humble-brag._

---

## "Still thin" note
_A short, honest heads-up on what's missing or shallow after this session — framed as a heads-up, not homework. This is what the next session (or the account manager) should chase down._
